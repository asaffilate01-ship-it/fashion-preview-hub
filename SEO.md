# KALËTHON SEO & directory plan

## On-site (done in code)

- Sitewide title template, description, geo-expanded keyword set, Open Graph + X cards, favicon (SVG/ICO/Apple touch), PWA manifest, robots and sitemap.
- Organization / WebSite / Brand structured data sitewide; Product + Offer on sport collections; CollectionPage, BreadcrumbList and FAQPage on market pages.
- Market landing pages at `/delivery-to/<slug>` with unique titles, descriptions, keywords, canonical, delivery/duties/currency detail and internal links:
  - **USA:** New York, Boston, Miami, Washington DC, Chicago, Los Angeles, Seattle
  - **UK:** United Kingdom (England, Scotland, Wales, Northern Ireland)
  - **EU:** all 27 member states, each with its own page
  - **Middle East:** United Arab Emirates (Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah)
- Hub page `/delivery-to` linked from the homepage footer and included in the sitemap.

## Competitor context (Semrush, US database)

Semrush has no organic data for kalethon.com yet — the domain is new, so all rankings must be earned.
Head terms held by Lacoste, Castore and lululemon (`lacoste` 246k/mo, `polo` 201k/mo, `sweatpants` 135k/mo)
are big-site territory. The realistic path is long-tail: "premium British tennis polo", "padel clothing Dubai",
"contrast collar polo UK", "modest sportswear UAE" — which is what the market and sport pages target.
